ODILIA GLOBAL

Verify Location & Take Selfie

Odilia Global Private Limited · Attendance capture

01 Share precise location

Allow location permission when your browser requests it. High-accuracy location is required; the portal will reject approximate or unavailable coordinates.

For best accuracy, use your phone with Location Services enabled, preferably near a window or outside. Do not use approximate-location mode.

02 Take front-camera selfie

Your selfie must be taken now. Uploading an existing photo is not available. Allow your browser to use your camera.

03 Return evidence to portal

Once your GPS location and selfie are ready, send them to the original attendance window to confirm the punch.

Your location and selfie are transmitted to the company's attendance portal after you select Send. Closing this window cancels the capture.
